YOUR INFORMATION / OUR POLICY

Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains how The Courier Link, LLC ("Courier Link," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit courierlinkllc.com, contact us, or arrange delivery services.

Information you provide

When you contact us or arrange a delivery, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, pickup and delivery addresses, recipient contact details, delivery instructions, and other information you choose to provide. We also retain correspondence and delivery records.

How we use information

We use customer information to arrange, coordinate, and complete deliveries, respond to pricing inquiries and questions, and communicate with customers and the people involved in their deliveries.

Who receives information

We do not sell customer information. We share customer information only with employees, drivers, contractors, and delivery partners who need it to carry out deliveries or related communications. We do not share it with unrelated parties for their marketing or advertising.

The technology providers used to host this website, handle email, and provide the AI tools described below also process information as part of providing those services. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Use of artificial intelligence

We may use customer information with artificial intelligence (AI) tools to assist with delivery-related work and customer communications. This may include organizing delivery details, summarizing inquiries, and drafting responses.

When we use third-party AI tools, information submitted to those tools may be processed by their providers. How those providers use and retain information, including whether it may be used to train or improve AI models, depends on the provider’s terms, privacy policy, and the settings in use.

For questions about our use of AI with your information, contact contact@courierlink.llc.

How long we keep information

We retain customer information indefinitely unless we receive a deletion request. To request deletion, email contact@courierlink.llc and describe the information you want deleted.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Information that we are legally required to retain may be kept for the required period. This retention statement describes our customer records; hosting and email providers may have separate retention practices for their systems.

Website hosting and tracking

This website does not include customer accounts, online forms, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. The website code does not set cookies or use browser storage.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. Learn more in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Email and telephone links open your email application or calling service. If you contact us, your communication is processed through the relevant email or telephone providers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or practices change. The effective date above shows when this version took effect.

Contact us

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact The Courier Link, LLC at contact@courierlink.llc.
